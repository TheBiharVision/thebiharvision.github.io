About The Bihar Vision

Honest conversations, rooted in Bihar.

The Bihar Vision is a Hindi/Bhojpuri YouTube podcast built around interviews and conversations covering culture, education, and entertainment relevant to Bihar and its people.

Our Mission

To create a space for unscripted, respectful conversations that reflect the real voices, ideas, and creativity connected to Bihar — for an audience of all age groups, depending on the episode's content.

Origin Story

The Bihar Vision was founded by Ritesh Sharma with the goal of building a long-form interview platform centered on Bihar's culture, education, and entertainment — presented in the region's own languages, Hindi and Bhojpuri.

Editorial Values

  • Represent guests and topics accurately and fairly.
  • Disclose sponsorships or paid placements where they occur.
  • Avoid fabricating quotes, statistics, or claims.
  • Correct factual errors transparently (see our Editorial Policy).

Topics We Cover

Interview, Culture, Education, and Entertainment — presented primarily in Hindi/Bhojpuri with some English-language content.

Production Approach

Episodes are recorded as long-form conversations and published directly to our YouTube channel, then indexed on this website with supporting written context.

Intended Audience

Content is intended for a general audience across age groups; individual episode suitability depends on its specific subject matter.
