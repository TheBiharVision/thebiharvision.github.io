1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites function properly, improve user experience, and provide information to website owners. This Cookie Policy explains how The Bihar Vision uses cookies on this website.

3. Third-Party Cookies

When you interact with embedded YouTube videos on our episode pages, YouTube (Google LLC) may set its own cookies in accordance with its privacy policy. We use the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com embed domain where possible to limit cookie usage until you actively interact with the player. We do not control cookies set by YouTube, Instagram, or Facebook when you visit or interact with their platforms directly.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control, block, or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Please note that blocking cookies entirely may affect certain website functionality, including embedded video playback. Most browsers allow you to view, manage, and delete cookies through their settings or privacy menu.

5. Consent

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable Indian law, because this site currently uses only strictly necessary cookies and conditionally-loaded third-party video cookies, a full cookie consent banner is not yet implemented. If analytics or advertising cookies are introduced in the future, a consent mechanism compliant with applicable law will be added before such cookies are activated.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in the cookies we use or applicable law. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last revised" date above. Please also see our Privacy Policy for related information on how we handle personal data.
