1. Who We Are

This website, The Bihar Vision (thebiharvision.github.io), is operated in connection with The Bihar Vision YouTube channel, founded by Ritesh Sharma and hosted by Alok Prasad. For queries related to this policy, please reach out via our Contact page or email thebiharvision@gmail.com, based in Patna, Bihar, India.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact form submissions: name, email address, subject, and message content, used solely to respond to your inquiry submitted via our Contact page.
  • Hosting logs: GitHub Pages, our hosting provider, may collect standard server logs (e.g. IP address, browser type) as part of its infrastructure. We do not control this data directly — see GitHub's own privacy documentation.
  • Cookies: see our Cookie Policy for full details.
  • Embedded YouTube videos: when you play an embedded video, YouTube (Google LLC) may set cookies and collect data per its own privacy policy. We use the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com embed domain where possible.

3. How We Use Information

We use contact form data only to respond to inquiries received through our Contact page. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties. We do not currently run third-party analytics (see Section 5).

4. Legal Basis for Processing (DPDP Act, 2023)

In accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), we process your personal data only on the basis of your free, informed, and specific consent, given when you voluntarily submit information through our Contact page. You may withdraw your consent at any time by emailing thebiharvision@gmail.com.

5. Contact Form Handling

Depending on configuration, our contact form either (a) submits via a configured third-party form provider, or (b) opens your email client with a pre-filled message addressed to our business email. No message content is stored on our servers beyond what a configured third-party provider retains under its own policy.

6. Analytics

Analytics are not currently enabled on this site. If enabled in the future, this policy and our Cookie Policy will be updated accordingly, along with a consent mechanism where required under applicable Indian law.

7. Third-Party Services

This site embeds content from and links to: YouTube/Google, Instagram (Meta), Facebook (Meta), and GitHub Pages (hosting). Each operates under its own privacy policy, over which we have no control.

8. Data Retention

Contact form messages are retained only as long as necessary to respond to and resolve your inquiry, or per the retention policy of our configured form provider, if any, and in line with the data minimisation principles under the DPDP Act, 2023.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable security practices and procedures, as mandated under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and associated rules, to protect your information. However, no website can guarantee absolute security. Please avoid sending sensitive personal data via our contact form.

10. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you (as a "Data Principal") have the right to:

  • Access information about the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • Request erasure of your personal data, where no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected;
  • Withdraw consent at any time;
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity;
  • Register a grievance regarding the processing of your personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Grievance Officer via our Contact page or email thebiharvision@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe as required by law.

11. Children's Privacy

This site is not specifically directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without verifiable parental or guardian consent, as required under the DPDP Act, 2023.

12. Cross-Border Data Transfer

As some third-party services we use (such as YouTube and GitHub Pages) may process data outside India, such transfers are governed by the respective third party's own privacy and security practices, consistent with applicable Indian law.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last revised" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
