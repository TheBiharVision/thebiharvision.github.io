We use contact form data only to respond to inquiries. We do not sell personal information. We do not currently run third-party analytics (see Section 5).

4. Contact Form Handling

Depending on configuration, our contact form either (a) submits via a configured third-party form provider, or (b) opens your email client with a pre-filled message addressed to our business email. No message content is stored on our servers beyond what a configured third-party provider retains under its own policy.

5. Analytics

Analytics are not currently enabled on this site. If enabled in the future, this policy and our Cookie Policy will be updated accordingly, along with a consent mechanism where required.

6. Third-Party Services

This site embeds content from and links to: YouTube/Google, Instagram (Meta), Facebook (Meta), and GitHub Pages (hosting). Each operates under its own privacy policy.

7. Data Retention

Contact form messages are retained only as long as necessary to respond to and resolve your inquiry, or per the retention policy of our configured form provider, if any.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no website can guarantee absolute security. Please avoid sending sensitive personal data via our contact form.
