Our Commitment

The Bihar Vision aims to make this website usable by as many people as possible, following WCAG 2.2 Level AA as our implementation target.

What We've Implemented

  • Semantic HTML and a logical heading structure.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation, search, and forms.
  • Visible focus indicators on all interactive elements.
  • Sufficient color contrast within our monochromatic palette.
  • Accessible form labels and inline error messages.
  • Support for reduced-motion preferences.
  • Descriptive link text and image alt attributes.

Video Captions & Transcripts

Caption availability depends on YouTube's auto-generated or creator-uploaded captions on each video. Transcripts are published on episode pages only when available and authorized — see individual episode pages for status.

Known Limitations

As a continually improving site, some third-party embeds (e.g. the YouTube player) are controlled by Google and may not fully match our accessibility standards.

Reporting Accessibility Issues

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please contact thebiharvision@gmail.com with the page URL and a description of the issue. We will make reasonable efforts to address it.
