Accessibility Statement

Effective date: 16 October 2026.
Last revised: 16 October 2026.

1. Our Commitment

The Bihar Vision is committed to making this website usable by as many people as possible, including people with disabilities. We follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA as our implementation target.

2. What We've Implemented

  • Semantic HTML and a logical heading structure.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation, search, and forms.
  • Visible focus indicators on all interactive elements.
  • Sufficient color contrast within our monochromatic palette.
  • Accessible form labels and inline error messages.
  • Support for reduced-motion preferences.
  • Descriptive link text and image alt attributes.

3. Video Captions & Transcripts

Caption availability depends on YouTube's auto-generated or creator-uploaded captions on each video. Transcripts are published on episode pages only when available and authorized — see individual episode pages on our Episodes section for status.

4. Known Limitations

As a continually improving site, some third-party embeds (e.g. the YouTube player) are controlled by Google and may not fully match our accessibility standards. We will continue to monitor and improve accessibility as our content and platform evolve.

5. Compatibility

This website is designed to be compatible with recent versions of major browsers and assistive technologies, including screen readers. We recommend using the latest version of your browser for the best experience.

6. Ongoing Efforts

Accessibility is an ongoing effort. We periodically review our website's content and design, consistent with our Editorial Policy, to identify and address accessibility barriers.

7. Reporting Accessibility Issues

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this website, please contact us via our Contact page or email thebiharvision@gmail.com with the page URL and a description of the issue. We will make reasonable efforts to address it promptly.

8. Changes to This Statement

We may update this Accessibility Statement periodically to reflect improvements made to the website. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last revised" date above.
