Editorial Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026.
Last revised: 16 October 2026.

1. Our Editorial Commitment

The Bihar Vision is committed to producing honest, respectful, and accurate conversations covering culture, education, and entertainment relevant to Bihar. This Editorial Policy outlines the standards and practices we follow in producing our content.

2. Research & Source Verification

We aim to verify factual claims discussed in episodes where reasonably possible. Guest statements and opinions are attributed to the guest and are not independently fact-checked unless explicitly stated otherwise. Views expressed by guests do not necessarily reflect the views of The Bihar Vision — see our Disclaimer for more details.

3. Corrections Policy

If a factual error is identified in a published episode page or article, we will correct it promptly and note the correction date on the relevant page. To report an error, please contact us via our Contact page or email thebiharvision@gmail.com.

4. Guest Representation

Guests are represented based on verified public information and their own statements during the recorded conversation. We do not fabricate guest biographies, credentials, or quotes. Guest profiles published on this website reflect information shared by the guest or sourced from publicly available, verifiable information.

5. Sponsorship & Paid Placement Disclosure

Any sponsored episode, segment, or paid placement will be clearly disclosed in the episode description and on its corresponding website page. No undisclosed paid placements are made. This is consistent with our Disclaimer regarding sponsorships and affiliate links.

6. Independence & Conflicts of Interest

Our hosts and founders aim to conduct conversations independently and in good faith. Where a host, founder, or guest has a material personal or financial connection relevant to the topic discussed, we aim to disclose this where reasonably practicable.

7. AI-Assisted Content Practices

Portions of this website's structure, design, and templates may be developed with the assistance of AI tools. However, factual claims, quotes, transcripts, and guest information published on this site are not AI-generated or fabricated — they are sourced directly from verified episode content and information supplied by guests or our editorial team.

8. Content Language & Audience

Our content is presented primarily in Hindi/Bhojpuri, with some English-language content, intended for a general audience across age groups. Individual episode suitability depends on its specific subject matter.

9. Relationship to Our Other Policies

This Editorial Policy should be read together with our Disclaimer and Copyright Policy, which govern how we handle opinions, sponsorships, and third-party content ownership respectively.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Editorial Policy periodically to reflect changes in our content practices. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last revised" date above.

11. Contact

For editorial queries, corrections, or feedback, please reach out via our Contact page or email thebiharvision@gmail.com.
