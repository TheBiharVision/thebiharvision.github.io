Research & Source Verification

We aim to verify factual claims discussed in episodes where reasonably possible. Guest statements and opinions are attributed to the guest and not independently fact-checked unless stated.

Corrections

If a factual error is identified in a published episode page, we will correct it and note the correction date. To report an error, contact thebiharvision@gmail.com.

Guest Representation

Guests are represented based on verified public information and their own statements during the recorded conversation. We do not fabricate guest biographies, credentials, or quotes.

Sponsorship Disclosure

Any sponsored episode or segment will be clearly disclosed in the episode description and on its website page. No undisclosed paid placements are made.

AI-Assisted Content Practices

Portions of this website's structure and templates may be developed with AI assistance. Factual claims, quotes, transcripts, and guest information are not AI-generated or fabricated — they are sourced directly from verified episode content and supplied information.
