Founder

Ritesh Sharma

Ritesh Sharma is the founder of The Bihar Vision. He started the channel with the aim of creating a dedicated space for long-form interviews and conversations centered on Bihar's culture, education, and entertainment landscape, presented primarily in Hindi and Bhojpuri.

Role at The Bihar Vision

As founder, Ritesh Sharma oversees the channel's direction and editorial vision, working alongside host Alok Prasad to shape the topics and guests featured in each episode.

Vision for the Channel

The Bihar Vision was built around the idea that Bihar's culture, people, and ideas deserve thoughtful, unscripted representation — told through honest conversations rather than a predetermined narrative.
