Host

Alok Prasad

Alok Prasad hosts The Bihar Vision, leading interviews and conversations with guests from across Bihar's cultural, educational, and entertainment landscape. He conducts each episode primarily in Hindi and Bhojpuri.

Role in the Podcast

As the primary host, Alok Prasad leads guest conversations, shapes episode discussions, and presents each interview to the channel's audience.
